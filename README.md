One line, 200 characters at most, roasting a smart fridge — its 3 a.m.
self-update, its forgotten ice maker, and the life coach my leftovers
never asked for, who now bills by the hour.

## The line

> My smart fridge updated itself at 3 a.m., forgot how to make ice, and now texts me 'I'm learning' every time I open the door. My leftovers have a life coach who bills by the hour.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib-only, deterministic) writes the line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/fridge-roast-out node main.ts
cat /tmp/fridge-roast-out/entry.txt
```
